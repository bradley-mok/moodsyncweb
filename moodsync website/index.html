About us

MoodSync is a music app that helps you find songs based on how you are feeling. Instead of searching for music manually, the app understands your mood in different ways.

Your face

Use your camera to detect your facial expression and estimate your mood.

Your pick

Choose an emoji that matches how you feel, or select your environment: gym, home, or a quiet library.

Your well-being

Feeling sad? It suggests calming or uplifting songs. At the gym, it suggests energetic music.

MoodSync makes listening to music more personal while helping you become more aware of your emotional well-being. It also supports:

  • Personalized playlist creation
  • Customization of your preferences
  • Music for reflective or recovery moments

Features

Mood detection

Camera-based facial analysis, with heart rate readings where available, generates a playlist for how you feel.

Emoji mood picks

Tap the emoji that matches your mood and get music to fit it.

Environment playlists

Tell MoodSync where you are, such as the gym, home, or a library, and the music adapts.

Coping mode

Set a goal, for example from sad to happy, and get music that lifts your mood.

Auto playlists

Recommendations built from your listening history, your environment, and your detected emotion.

Mood history

A 14-day timeline and mood patterns, so you can reflect on how you have been feeling.

Playlists and playback

Build playlists, play songs, and follow what is playing in the Now Playing view.

Search, library and sharing

Search songs, artists, and albums, save favorites, and share tracks with friends.
